# docode Support

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, open an issue at:

https://github.com/carrieluuu/docode/issues

When reporting a problem, include:

- Your Chrome version and operating system.
- The action you were attempting, such as Insert, Update in Docs, or Smart Paste.
- Whether automatic insertion worked or manual paste recovery appeared.
- The selected or detected programming language.
- Reproduction steps and a screenshot with private document content removed.

Do not include confidential code, private document links, passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
